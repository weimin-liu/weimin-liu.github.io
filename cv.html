Research Interests

Organic geochemist using molecular fingerprints to trace biogeochemical processes in modern oceans, recent sediments, and ancient reservoirs. Experienced in big-data analysis combining high-resolution mass spectrometry with machine learning and co-occurrence network approaches. Develops open-source, user-friendly software for streamlined analysis of scientific data.

Research Experience
